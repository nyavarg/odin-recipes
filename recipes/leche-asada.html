“Leche Asada” from Gato, tipico chileno

Description

Quick and tasty dessert for any occasion

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 500 ml of milk
  • 0.25 cups of sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 100 grams of sugar
  • 50 ml of cold water

Steps

  1. Mix in a pot the 100 grams of sugar with the 50 ml of cold water
  2. Put the pot to low heat
  3. Mix in a bowl eggs, milk, 0.25 cups of sugar and cinnamon
  4. Softly stir without making bubbles
  5. Drop the caramel on a pudding bowl and then the previous mix
  6. Put the pudding bowl in the oven (previously heated to 180°C) for 30-35 minutes or until the surface is golden.
  7. Take it out the oven and let it cool, then serve it
