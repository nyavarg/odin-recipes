Gnocci‘s recipe from Love and Lemons

Description

“These homemade gnocchi are soft, pillowy, and light. Served with a flavorful sauce, they’re absolutely delicious”.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes and all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg
  • Sea salt

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F
  2. Use a fork to poke holes in the potatoes. Place on a baking sheet and bake for 45-60 minutes.
  3. When they are cool enough to handle, but still warm, peel off and then mash with a fork.
  4. Transfer the potatoes to a lightly floured surface and form them into a wide, thin mound. Drizzle with the egg and sprinkle with the salt and a third of the flour. Use your hands to lift and fold the potatoes into themselves to incorporate the egg and flour.
  5. Continue adding flour a little at time and kneading and folding the dough until it’s soft and pillowy, but not sticky
  6. Form the dough into a ball and cut it into 8 equal pieces. Dust your work surface and your hands with more flour and roll one piece into a rope about 1-inch in diameter. Cut it into ¾-inch pieces and transfer them to a floured plate or baking sheet. Repeat with the remaining dough.
  7. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Set a colander over a large bowl nearby. Add a third of the gnocchi to the boiling water and cook for about 2 minutes, or until they float. Use a slotted spoon to lift the gnocchi out of the boiling water and into the colander, then repeat twice with the remaining gnocchi.
  8. Serve with marinara sauce or pesto, or try another of the serving suggestions in the blog post above.
