‘Arroz con leche’s recipe from IANSA

Description

Sweet and easy to make dessert. Your family will love it!

Ingredients

  • 1 liter of milk
  • 100 grams of rice
  • Peel of 1 orange
  • Peel of 1 lemon
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 70 grams of sugar

Steps

  1. Put everything but the sugar on a big pot
  2. Put the pot on low-medium heat and stir from time to time
  3. For 20-25 minutes keep the heat slow, avoid boiling
  4. Put the sugar on the mix and keep it another 10 minutes on the heat, stirring regularly
  5. Turn off the heat and let it cool before going to the fridge
  6. After 2-3 hours it will be cool enough to serve. Enjoy!
